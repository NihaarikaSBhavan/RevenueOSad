## **6\. Product Architecture & Feature List**

The product is structured across 17 modules , organized into four key areas: Customer-Facing AI, Automation Core, Staff Surfaces, and Platform Foundation.

### **Part A: Customer-Facing AI (Modules 1–4)**

#### 01 Lead Capture

Three entry points feed into one unified pipeline:

* Public Enquiry Page: A no-login page that renders labels, service lists, and fields from workspace config, automatically stamping campaign attribution (e.g., `?source=`).  
* Staff Addition: Team members can manually add a lead from the CRM in seconds.  
* WhatsApp Automation: Any customer messaging the business on WhatsApp becomes a lead automatically.  
* Phone Number Normalization: Phone numbers are automatically formatted to international standards at the point of entry to prevent downstream messaging and calling failures.

#### 02 AI Concierge

A conversational agent that operates on WhatsApp:

* Grounding: Answers questions based on the tenant's own knowledge base, rate card, real services, real prices, and real branches.  
* Intelligent Tool-Calling: The model doesn't just talk; it acts by reading availability, proposing bookings, and updating records.  
* 24/7 Availability: Provides instant responses in the brand's configured tone.  
* Escalation: Automatically escalates to a human the moment a lead qualifies, preventing bot loops.

#### 03 Chat → CRM Write-back

Eliminates manual data entry by automatically extracting information from conversations:

* Automatic Extraction: Extracts requirements, budget, preferred branch, occasion, and follow-up time from customer conversations.  
* Evidence & Confidence: Each field is written with the customer's own words attached as the source quote and a confidence score.  
* Dynamic Updates: Any real change triggers a regeneration of the 360 summary and files a next-action task for the owner.

#### 04 Voice: AI Calls & Call Assist

Handles both outbound and inbound voice interactions:

* Outbound AI Calls: The routing journey places a real voice call to warm a lead that isn't hot yet (once per lead, never a nag loop), returning the call's outcome to the thread.  
* Staff Call Assist: Click-to-call from any contact card with a live panel showing real-time transcript, AI assist, and the customer's full history during the call.

### **Part B: Automation Core (Modules 5–8)**

#### 05 Lead Scoring & Routing

Prioritizes leads based on a live, configurable score:

* Configurable Signals: Builds a score from signals like category fit, engagement, recency, source quality, and conversion intent.  
* Live Recalculation: The score updates on every conversation turn, routing Hot leads to human handover while the AI continues to work Warm/Cold leads.  
* Transparency: The signal breakdown is visible on every contact, making the score an argument, not a mystery.

#### 06 Journey Engine

The nervous system that reacts to domain events:

* Event-Driven: Reacts to occurrences like lead created, message received, score changed, or appointment booked.  
* Configurable Flows: Journeys are config-defined flows that can send messages, wait, or ask an agent to perform an action.  
* Deduplication & Permissions: Every automated send is deduplicated, and each step's tool access is an explicit grant.

#### 07 Approvals & Autonomy

The deliberate human moment for oversight:

* Autonomy Dial: Every AI-proposed action passes through an autonomy dial, allowing it to be queued for one-click approve (low autonomy) or execute automatically (high autonomy).  
* Supervised to Autonomous: New flows start supervised and graduate to autonomous when the team is comfortable.

#### 08 Booking, Calendar & Reminders

End-to-end scheduling and after-care:

* Real Availability: Reads the assigned team member's real availability inside branch opening hours.  
* Conflict-Checked Proposals: Proposes a conflict-checked slot, snapping to a valid one if the customer's ask collides.  
* One-Click Booking: One click books the appointment, which appears on the CRM calendar with an AI badge and audit trail.  
* Automated After-Care: The customer receives a tap-to-add calendar invite on WhatsApp, a 24-hour reminder, a thank-you after completed visits, and a recovery task within four hours for no-shows.

### **Part C: Staff Surfaces (Modules 9–13)**

#### 09 Contact 360

A single page that makes a staff member instantly current on any customer:

* AI-Maintained Summary: An AI-maintained summary of the customer.  
* Captured Profile: The full profile with each field's source quote and confidence score.  
* Live Temperature: The live lead score with its signal breakdown.  
* Full Timeline: Messages, calls, appointments, journey actions, and journey history showing what automation has done and what it plans next.

#### 10 Inbox & Handover

Mirrors every WhatsApp thread in the CRM:

* Full Thread Mirroring: Customer, AI, and staff turns are all visible.  
* One-Click Takeover: A human can take over any conversation at any point.  
* Trackable Notifications: Handover pings reach the staff member's own WhatsApp and are logged in a dedicated staff thread, each ping linked back to the lead.

#### 11 Tasks & Today Feed

The AI files work for humans like a good assistant:

* Auto-Generated Tasks: Creates next-action tasks after meaningful conversations, booking tasks when no slot fits, and recovery tasks after a no-show.  
* Prioritized Feed: The Today feed collects what actually needs a person now.

#### 12 Deals & Pipeline

Revenue attached to relationships:

* Configurable Stages: Deals move through configurable stages linked to contacts and appointments.  
* Live Pipeline: Pipeline numbers reflect what actually happened rather than what someone remembered to log.  
* AI-Assisted Stage Moves: The AI can propose stage moves (through Approvals) when the conversation warrants one.

#### 13 Dashboard & Analytics

Reports the funnel and, distinctively, what the AI did:

* Funnel Metrics: Leads by source and temperature, bookings, and revenue.  
* AI Contribution Metrics: Fields auto-filled, visits pre-booked, and conversations handled without staff.

### **Part D: Platform Foundation (Modules 14–17)**

#### 14 Catalog & Knowledge Base

The tenant's commercial reality as data:

* Service Rate Card & Products: Manages services (e.g., 37 services in the live deployment) and retail products (e.g., 16 SKUs).  
* Branches & Knowledge Base: Contains branches with opening hours and knowledge-base articles explicitly marked as usable by the AI.

#### 15 Workspace Config & Packs

The strategic core: one generic engine, and every industry is a configuration pack:

* Configurable Vocabulary: Tenant's vocabulary, captured fields, lead sources, personas, scoring bands, currency, locale, and timezone are all config.  
* Rows, Not Code: Onboarding a business or a whole new vertical is loading rows: services, fields, tone, rules.

#### 16 Templates & Messaging Compliance

Ensures all outbound messaging respects WhatsApp Business rules:

* 24-Hour Window: Inside the customer-service window, the AI converses freely; outside, only preapproved templates go out.  
* Enforcement: When a send is not permitted, the system records an honest "not sent" instead of pretending.

#### 17 Multi-tenancy, Security & Quality

Ensures isolation, permissions, and auditability:

* Row-Level Security: Postgres with row-level security isolates every tenant across all 78 tables.  
* Permissions: Seats and roles gate what each user and each AI action may do.  
* Provenance & Audit: Every AI-written value carries provenance; every automated action is attributed and auditable.  
* Quality Enforcement: One bootstrap path for dev, CI, and production; append-only checksummed migrations; CI gates on vocabulary and structure; and 889 automated tests that run against a dedicated test database.

## **16\. Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Q1: What is Customer Lifecycle OS?**

Customer Lifecycle OS is the Enterprise AI Operating System for Complex Industries. It automates the complete customer journey—from lead capture to booked visit and follow-up—with human involvement condensed to exactly three clicks.

### **Q2: How is this different from a traditional CRM?**

Traditional CRMs are "systems of record"—they track what happened. Customer Lifecycle OS is a "system of execution"—it doesn't just store data, it acts on it. It captures leads, handles conversations, books appointments, sends reminders, and follows up—all automatically.

### **Q3: Does this replace my existing CRM?**

Yes. Customer Lifecycle OS is designed to replace legacy CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho. It handles the complete customer lifecycle from end to end, with no need for separate tools or manual data entry.

### **Q4: How long does implementation take?**

Traditional enterprise software implementations take 6–12 months. Our "configuration, not code" architecture enables implementation in 14–30 days for mid-market customers, and 30–60 days for enterprise customers.

### **Q5: How much human involvement is required?**

Exactly three clicks per customer lifecycle. Click 1: Approve the booking. Click 2: Approve the message (if supervised). Click 3: Mark the task complete. Everything else runs automatically.

### **Q6: How does the Autonomy Dial work?**

Every AI-proposed action passes through an autonomy dial. At low autonomy, actions are queued for one-click approval. At high autonomy, actions execute automatically and remain visible and reversible. New flows start supervised and graduate to autonomous when the team is comfortable.

### **Q7: How do I trust the AI?**

Every AI action carries evidence, provenance, and is reversible. Every field in the CRM has a source quote and confidence score attached. And the Autonomy Dial lets you start supervised and gradually grant autonomy as trust is earned.

### **Q8: Can this handle my industry?**

Yes. Customer Lifecycle OS is built on a "configuration, not code" architecture. Every industry is a configuration pack—rows in the database, never code. Onboarding a new vertical is loading rows, not shipping code.

### **Q9: What integrations are available?**

Customer Lifecycle OS integrates via API with complementary SaaS like Calendly, Zapier, Slack, and others. We also work with System Integrators for enterprise deployment.

### **Q10: Is my data secure?**

Yes. Postgres with row-level security isolates every tenant across all 78 tables. Every AI-written value carries provenance; every automated action is attributed and auditable. 889 automated tests run against a dedicated test database.
