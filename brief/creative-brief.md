# Creative Brief: Revenue OS 30-second film

Source of truth: the three files in /brief. The Master Positioning Document governs all claims and wording. The Film System governs structure, voiceover, visuals and the end card. Where the Product Context Document disagrees ("CRM Killer", "94%", "100x", "replaces your CRM"), the positioning doc wins.

## Product
- On screen: REVENUE OS, endorsed by TheVertical.ai
- Category: Customer Revenue OS
- One idea: "Every lead worked. Every customer grown."
- Contrast line (the only one): "Your CRM records what happened. Revenue OS makes it happen."
- Positioning: sits on top of the CRM and systems already in place. No rip-out. Live in weeks.

## Audience
CEO, Founder, Head of Revenue, Head of Sales at businesses with real digital lead inflow: edtech, healthcare, real estate, insurance, D2C, B2B SaaS. India-first.

## The three revenue states
1. Not yet won: leads paid for that go cold, get called late, or are never reached
2. Won but not grown: the second, third and fourth purchase never asked for
3. Won but at risk: customers drifting quietly toward churn

## Product moments to show (all demo-true)
- Lead capture with clean number and source attribution
- WhatsApp concierge answering with real pricing and branch
- Mid-chat language switch (English to Hindi)
- Chat-to-record write-back with the customer's quote and a confidence score
- Live lead score with signal breakdown (intent, engagement, recency, source)
- Conflict-checked booking that snaps to a valid slot
- The 3 human clicks: approve booking, approve message, mark task complete
- Auto calendar invite, 24h reminder, thank-you
- Cross-sell next-best-action
- Churn signal caught and acted on
- Autonomy dial: Supervised to Autonomous
- Audit trail with timestamps

## Allowed on-screen numbers
3 clicks · 24/7 · 100% of leads worked · "within minutes". No other percentages or multiples.

## Banned words (screen and voice)
chatbot, voicebot, bot, CRM (as what we are), AI-powered, AI-enabled, AI-assisted, platform, solution, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, transform, revolutionise, disrupt, 10x, 100x, digital transformation, unlock, supercharge, turbocharge, democratise, living system, alive, nervous system, brain, workflow automation, insights

## Brand
- #0177FF primary (the OS acting)
- #051234 deep navy background
- #FE772C accent, used sparingly (hot lead, the clicks, the CTA)
- #111827 neutral
- Font: IBM Plex Sans (numbers in IBM Plex Mono)
- Tone: confident, direct, calm. Never trailer-excited.

## Film rules
- 30 seconds: 5s tension (one shot) → 20s one continuous demonstration → 5s close + end card
- Exactly 3 voiceover lines
- Captions burned in; must work with sound off
- End card frame 1 (2s, in silence on drumbeats): REAL TIME. / UNDER POLICY. / ON YOUR INFRASTRUCTURE.
- End card frame 2 (2s): TheVertical.ai logo + "Part of the TheVertical.ai Enterprise OS" + CTA
